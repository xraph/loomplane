# Security

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please don't open a public issue. Report it privately through GitHub's [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/xraph/loomplane/security/advisories/new) for this repository, with what you found, how to reproduce it, and what you think it lets someone do. We'll confirm we've got it, and tell you what we'll do and when.

## How Loomplane protects what it holds

[docs/security.md](docs/security.md) describes it: who can do what, where credentials and secrets live, what never runs on a Loomplane machine, and how it's tested.
